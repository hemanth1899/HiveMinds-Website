Fee architecture & unit economics

How our open-book model works

Zero land-holding overhead

We never buy speculative land or pass debt interest onto homeowners. Clients fund construction through 6 stage-wise milestone tranches unlocked only after prior phase inspection.

Flat 20% management & engineering fee

Instead of hiding 35–40% markups inside material bills, we pass wholesale vendor rates directly to you and charge a fixed 20% fee (e.g., ₹13.20 Lakhs on a standard 2,400 sq.ft. Signature build of ₹66L base cost).

Independent legal & soil vetting

Optional flat ₹50,000 due-diligence desk for plot-seekers needing A-Khata title verification and soil testing prior to purchase.
